# 04 — Focus Tap: Pond Juice and Sound

## What to build

Make the catch and the jar feel alive: the tongue, a sparkle at the target, fireflies lighting in the jar, a soft croak, a catch plop, and a fill chime. Keep the shared Trainer Music under it. No negative sound.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The tongue visibly reaches the tapped element and retracts
- [ ] A sparkle bursts at the catch point
- [ ] Each firefly lights with a small pop; the jar fill animates
- [ ] A catch plays a quiet plop and a soft croak
- [ ] Filling the jar plays a warm three-note chime
- [ ] The shared Trainer Music keeps playing under the effects
- [ ] All sound respects the existing setting
- [ ] Effects remain usable under reduced motion
- [ ] Navigating away stops the music and pending timers
- [ ] E2E or behavioral coverage for the jar count and the muted path
