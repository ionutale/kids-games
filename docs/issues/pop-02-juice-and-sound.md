# 02 — Pop: Juice and Sound

## What to build

Make every pop legible and collectible: a ripple where the bubble burst, minis popping into the jar, a star sticker on the rim, and the quiet pond sound pass. Giant cracks, star sparkles, and the fill chime should be distinct without becoming noisy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each pop leaves a short ripple at the burst point
- [ ] Surprises pop into the jar and settle visibly
- [ ] A star sticker appears when the jar fills or a star bubble pops
- [ ] Pop plays a water plop whose pitch rises through a chain
- [ ] Spawn plays a soft bubble-up at low volume
- [ ] Filling the jar plays a three-note chime
- [ ] No clock ticking and no negative sound
- [ ] Everything respects the existing sound setting
- [ ] Ripples, stickers and jar minis remain usable under reduced motion
- [ ] Navigating away stops the animation loop and pending timers
- [ ] E2E or behavioral coverage for the jar minis, star sticker and muted sound
