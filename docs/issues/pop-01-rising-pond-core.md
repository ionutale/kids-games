# 01 — Pop: Rising Pond Core

## What to build

Replace the emoji timer game with the Rising Pond: a portrait moonlit pond, rising bubbles with surprises inside, a jar that fills to the target, a moon arc for the 30-second clock, and a calm end card. Tap or swipe pops; giant and star bubbles add the two-tap and bonus layers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pond renders in portrait with lily pads, reeds and fireflies
- [ ] The first tap starts the round; bubbles rise from the water
- [ ] Tapping a bubble pops it; swiping pops every bubble crossed
- [ ] The pop pitch rises through a chain and resets on lift
- [ ] Surprise items fly into the jar and stay visible
- [ ] The jar fills by points; giant and star bubbles are worth two
- [ ] A giant bubble cracks on the first tap and bursts on the second
- [ ] A star bubble leaves a sticker on the jar
- [ ] The moon arc replaces the numeric countdown
- [ ] Reaching the target wins the level with the ladder actions
- [ ] Time ending short shows Good Try with Try again / Back, no star
- [ ] No negative sound; escaped bubbles have no penalty
- [ ] E2E covers start, tap, swipe, giant, star, win and good try
- [ ] Unit tests cover target/level config and pop values
- [ ] No new dependencies

## Blocked by

None — can start immediately
