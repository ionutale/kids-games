# 04 — Quick Count: Picnic Juice and Sound

## What to build

Make the flash and the basket feel alive: a soft count tick per object on the first levels, a berry plop on a correct answer, a basket-fill chime, and a clear visual fill. Keep the shared Trainer Music under it. No negative sound.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A soft tick plays once per object during the flash on levels 1–2 only
- [ ] No ticks play from level 3 upward
- [ ] A correct answer plays a quiet berry plop
- [ ] Filling the basket plays a warm three-note chime
- [ ] The berry visibly lights and the basket fill animates
- [ ] The shared Trainer Music keeps playing under the effects
- [ ] All sound respects the existing setting
- [ ] Effects remain usable under reduced motion
- [ ] Navigating away stops the music and pending timers
- [ ] E2E or behavioral coverage for the basket count and the muted path
